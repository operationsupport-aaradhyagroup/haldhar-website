Seasonal Solutions

Kharif, Rabi aur Zaid — Har Season ke Liye Solutions

Kharif Solutions (June–October)

High-performance paddy, maize, and soybean seeds; systemic insecticides for stem borer and leaf folder; broad-spectrum fungicides for blast and blight; and PGR support for better tillering and grain filling.

Rabi Solutions (October–March)

Premium wheat, mustard, and chickpea seeds; fertilizer blends for cold-weather root development; aphid and mite control for mustard; and micronutrient sprays for improved grain quality.

Zaid Solutions (March–June)

Fast-maturing vegetable and summer crop seeds; irrigation-compatible fertilizers; disease management for cucurbits; and heat-stress management with PGR products.
