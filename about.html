About HALDHAR

HALDHAR, a proud initiative by KRISHIDHAN AGRI BUSINESS PRIVATE LIMITED, was founded with a single conviction: every farmer, regardless of farm size, deserves access to quality agri-inputs and honest advice. Based in Jabalpur, Madhya Pradesh, we have been that trusted partner — from seed selection to harvest.

Our Mission — Empowering Farmers from Beej to Fasal

We exist to close the gap between what farmers need and what they can reliably access. From certified seeds and balanced fertilizers to expert pest management advice and PGR support — HALDHAR delivers agri solutions that work in the fields of Jabalpur, not just on paper.

Our promise is simple: when a farmer walks into our store or contacts us on WhatsApp, they leave with the right product, the right dosage guidance, and the confidence to protect and grow their crop.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide our daily operations and our relationship with the farming community.

Empowering Farmers

We believe that knowledge is as powerful as any input. That's why every product sale at HALDHAR comes with practical advice on correct application, timing, and dose — so farmers get the full benefit of what they buy.

Uncompromised Quality

We stock only genuine, verified products from reputed manufacturers. Counterfeit or substandard agri-inputs damage crops and erode trust. At HALDHAR, quality is non-negotiable — for every product, every time stage.

Affordability with Value

Profitability for farmers starts with cost-effective inputs. We curate our product range to offer the best performance-to-cost solutions available — not the most expensive options, but the most effective ones for local conditions.

Collaborative Growth

We grow when our farmer community grows. Our relationships with dealers, distributors, and farming families across Jabalpur district are built on long-term trust, not one-time transactions.

Sustainable Innovation

Indian agriculture faces evolving challenges — changing weather, new pest pressures, soil degradation. We stay current with new agri-tech developments and introduce sustainable, research-backed solutions suited to Madhya Pradesh's agro-climatic conditions.

Our Founder

Aman Sohaney

Aman Sohaney founded KRISHIDHAN AGRI BUSINESS PRIVATE LIMITED with a hands-on understanding of the real challenges Indian farmers face — from finding genuine seeds before the sowing window, to identifying a pest outbreak in time, to managing soil health across seasons.

With years of direct experience working across diverse farming landscapes in Madhya Pradesh, Aman brings a ground-level perspective that shapes every aspect of the business. His vision is not to sell products, but to deliver outcomes — healthier crops, better yields, and more profitable farms for the families we serve.

Under his leadership, the HALDHAR brand has grown into the most trusted agri-input store in Jabalpur — known for integrity, farmer-first thinking, and a team that genuinely cares about what happens after the purchase.

Aman's philosophy: build relationships, not just revenue. It's visible in how our team engages with every farmer, whether they're buying a single product or planning an entire season's input schedule.

What Our Team Stands For

Trustworthy

Committed

Farmer-Focused
